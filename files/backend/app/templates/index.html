    AI Training Logs (XGBoost + Whisper)
    
          
    
            How it works: Silero features → XGBoost decides HUMAN / MACHINE / IVR.
            If XGBoost says HUMAN but confidence is below your threshold,
            Whisper (speech→text) runs on that clip only — that is
            “XGBoost + Whisper on uncertain HUMAN”. MACHINE/IVR skip Whisper.
            Training Mark-as rows also get Whisper WAV→text when audio exists
            (or click Fill Whisper text / the 📝 button for older logs).
            Numbers are never forced — each dial is judged from its recording.
